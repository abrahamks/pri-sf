About

Persekutuan Reformed Indonesia San Francisco (PRI SF, or Indonesian Reformed Fellowship San Francisco) is a fellowship that began with a meeting by several Indonesians living in the San Francisco Bay Area, and was started by Rev. David Tong on October 13, 2011.

We gather every Saturday for Bible study (Pendalaman Alkitab) and seminars, held in Indonesian. Alongside the adult program, we offer classes for children — preschool (ages 3–5) and younger elementary (K–3rd grade) — using the Show Me Jesus curriculum.

As our name suggests, we hold to the core beliefs and doctrines of Reformed theology. Whether you're new to the Bay Area, looking for a spiritual home, or simply curious, we warmly invite you to join us this Saturday.
